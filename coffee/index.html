Roasted far
north of the bean belt

Coffee grows between 25°N and 30°S. Not one bean is grown in Sweden — yet 73 independent roasteries here buy it green, roast it, and sell it direct from their own webshops. They are spread across 16 län, from Ystad on the Baltic coast to Lycksele in Lapland, a thousand kilometres north. This is where they are.

About the list

73 Swedish coffee roasteries that run their own direct webshop. Descriptions and addresses come from the source dataset; every roastery here sells its own roast online.

Coordinates

Addresses were geocoded against OpenStreetMap via Nominatim and projected with a Web Mercator, the same projection used by ordinary web maps. Where only a town was listed, the pin sits on the town centre.

Latitude

The axis on the left of the map is a real scale — one tick per roastery, placed at its latitude. The dashed line is the Arctic Circle at 66°34′N. None of them reach it: the northernmost, in Lycksele, stops about 220 km short.
